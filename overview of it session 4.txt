1. List all 6 phases of SDLC in order with one line about each.

Planning – Identifies the project goals, requirements, resources, and timeline.
Requirement Analysis – Collects and analyzes what the users and system need.
Design – Creates the system structure, interface, database, and technical design.
Development – Developers write the code and build the software.
Testing – The software is tested to find and fix bugs and errors.
Deployment & Maintenance – The software is released to users and maintained with updates and fixes.

2. Write 3 differences between Agile and Waterfall models.

Agile
Development is done in small, repeated cycles.
Requirements can change during development.
Testing happens throughout the development process.

Waterfall
Development follows a fixed sequence of phases.
Requirements are usually fixed at the beginning.
Testing mainly happens after development is completed.


3. Name the 3 main Scrum roles and write one line about the responsibility of each.

Product Owner – Manages the product requirements and prioritizes the work.
Scrum Master – Helps the team follow Scrum practices and removes obstacles.
Development Team – Builds, tests, and delivers the product features.

4. What is a Sprint in Scrum? Mention its typical duration.

A Sprint is a fixed period in which the Scrum team works to complete a selected set of tasks and deliver a usable product increment.
Typical duration: 1–4 weeks, commonly 2 weeks.

5. What do these Jira board columns mean?

To Do – Tasks that are planned but have not been started.
In Progress – Tasks that are currently being worked on.
Done – Tasks that have been completed.
Backlog – A list of tasks, features, or requirements that are planned for future work.
